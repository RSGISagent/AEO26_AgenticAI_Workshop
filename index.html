Practical and Sovereign AI for Earth Observation

A hands-on agentic coding workshop at AEO26

Monday 9 November 2026 · 9:00 am – 12:30 pm · Nipaluna/Hobart, Lutruwita/Tasmania

Learn to work with an AI coding agent, Claude Code, by building something real with it. You describe what you want in plain English, the agent writes and runs the code, and you check the result. You don't need any coding experience. If you do code, there are harder tasks for you.

The example uses open Australian data: Sentinel-2 satellite images from before, just after and five years after the 2019–20 Black Summer fires near Braidwood, NSW, with fire-severity mapping and greater glider records.

Before you arrive (about 20 minutes)

You need a laptop with a web browser, a personal Claude account on the Pro plan or higher (the free plan doesn't include Claude Code on the web; prices), and a free personal GitHub account. Then follow the setup guide. In seven short steps you make your own private copy of the workshop repo.

Please use personal accounts, not work ones, and use the browser rather than the Claude Desktop app. No paid plan? Pair up with a neighbour on the day.
